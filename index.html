01 / ABOUT ME

From sales floors to cloud platforms.

With over a decade of expertise in sales and customer service, I've successfully transitioned into technical roles and I'm currently working as a Cloud Platform Engineer (DevOps) at FLYR.

I have hands-on knowledge of GCP, AWS and Azure, and I work daily with Git, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Ansible, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, ArgoCD, and CircleCI. I enjoy writing Bash scripts and creating automated workflows.

I prefer working on bigger projects so I can take my time and make them properly, not just done. I love writing documentation, guides and blog posts, and I'm a big fan of calisthenics and the occasional Rocket League rage-quit.
