# Five directions for Flint's screen

Real renders, not paintings: `lab.html?v=1..5` draws each one with canvas code
in the same situation (Flint speaking, two agents working), so the one you pick
can be turned into a face with the same zoom-into-the-team behaviour as The Core.

| # | Name | The voice is | The team is | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Nebula Core** | the outer wireframe sphere, a waveform ring around it, two tilted orbital rings with ticks and satellites, plus an inner shell | the dense core with labelled lead nodes and worker dots | the current Core, thickened: nebula, depth, chrome, readouts |
| 2 | **Iris** | a 180-bar radial equaliser around a ring, rotating segmented rings, a bezel with degrees, text running on an arc | a honeycomb: Command hex in the middle, one hex per department with its workers as dots | precise, instrument-like, teal and amber |
| 3 | **Orbital** | the sun: a corona of rays whose length is the waveform | planets on tilted orbits with moons (workers), an asteroid belt of tools | cinematic, warm, the most "living system" of the five |
| 4 | **Glass HUD** | a hologram globe with three sound bands wrapped around it | a real panel: the tree with running / idle per worker, an activity log, an oscilloscope, plan gauges, a timeline | dense, information-first, monochrome cyan |
| 5 | **Neural Bloom** | a magenta nucleus with thought streams bursting outward through a particle field | luminous orbs on an inner ring with their workers around them, linked to the centre | organic, soft, the least "UI" of the five |

Previews are in this folder (`v1.jpg` to `v5.jpg`). Zoom behaviour for each:
1 and 5 zoom into the core exactly like The Core; 2 opens the iris (the rings
part and the honeycomb expands into the chart); 3 flies into a planet's system;
4 already shows everything, and Z swaps the globe for the chart.

Pick a number and Flint gets that face; the other four stay here as options.
